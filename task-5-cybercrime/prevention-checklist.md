# Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist

## 🔐 General Safety Tips
- Do not click on suspicious links in emails or messages  
- Always verify website URLs before entering login details  
- Use strong and unique passwords for different accounts  
- Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever possible  
- Avoid sharing personal or sensitive information online  
- Keep your apps and devices updated regularly  
- Do not trust messages that create urgency or fear  
- Always verify unknown calls or messages before taking action
## 💰 UPI / Financial Safety (Important)
- Never share your OTP or UPI PIN with anyone
- Do not accept unknown payment requests on UPI apps

## 🚨 Awareness & Reporting
- Be cautious of messages creating urgency or fear
- Verify calls claiming to be from banks or officials
- Report cybercrime at: https://cybercrime.gov.in
- Helpline Number: 1930
